About Thinking Machines Lab
Thinking Machines Lab's mission is to empower humanity through advancing collaborative general intelligence. The team includes creators of ChatGPT, Character.ai, Mistral, PyTorch, and other prominent AI and open-source projects.

About Tinker
Tinker is a fine-tuning API enabling researchers and developers to customize open-weight models using their own data and algorithms, while Thinking Machines manages the distributed training infrastructure. The platform is expanding rapidly with new customers and use cases. Tinker exposes low-level training primitives (forward_backward, optim_step, sample, save_state) and uses LoRA for efficient post-training; the open-source Tinker Cookbook provides modern implementations of post-training methods (SFT, DPO, RLHF, RL environments, evals) on top of the API.
